The 15 minute ear routine
  1. Check the key1 min

    The app sets the root to your key of the week. Look at the Key tab so you know the seven chords.

  2. Hands on the colors4 min

    Pick one level in the library. Play each chord on piano, guitar and bass while saying its name.

  3. A/B pairs2 min

    Run two pairs in Chord colors with your eyes closed. Describe the difference like an EQ move.

  4. Name the numbers4 min

    Ten progressions. Say the numbers out loud before you tap.

  5. Find home2 min

    Ten bass notes. Hum home first, then sing up to the note.

  6. Real music2 min

    Put on a song, find home on your bass, and name one chord.
